About Kayla

Kayla discovered her love of photography in the darkroom of her Film Photography class in college. Times have changed, but her love of photography stays the same.

Specializing in capturing life's most precious moments, Kayla brings a unique artistic vision to every session. From the first breath of a newborn to the proud smile of a graduating senior, every moment tells a story worth preserving.
