About Kayla

Professional Portrait Photographer

My Story

Kayla discovered her love of photography in the darkroom of her Film Photography class in college. Times have changed, but her love of photography stays the same.

What started as a passion for capturing light and shadow on film has evolved into a deep understanding of how to tell stories through images. Kayla believes that every person has a unique story to tell, and her mission is to capture those authentic moments that make each individual special.

With years of experience in portrait photography, Kayla specializes in creating timeless images that families will treasure for generations. Her approach is relaxed and natural, allowing genuine emotions to shine through in every photograph.

When she's not behind the camera, Kayla can be found exploring new locations, studying the work of master photographers, or spending time with her own family - the people who inspire her most.
